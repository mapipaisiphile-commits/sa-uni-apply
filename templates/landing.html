🔥 NSFAS 2026 - Full Guide

NSFAS covers: Tuition + Accommodation + Books + Transport + Food

Documents You Need BEFORE Applying:
  • Certified ID copy (not older than 3 months)
  • Parent/guardian ID copies
  • Proof of income / SASSA letter / Affidavit
  • Consent form signed
  • Proof of residence
  • Grade 11 final + Grade 12 latest results
  • Disability certificate (if applicable)
NB: All certified copies must NOT be older than 3 months!
Who qualifies?
  • South African citizen
  • Combined household income less than R350,000 per year
  • SASSA grant recipients automatically qualify
  • Grade 9-12 already studying
